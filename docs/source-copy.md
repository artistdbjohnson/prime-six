- With Prime 6: Same size, clean and in a stackable box. Less than 5% moister. Sold by the LB so measurable food cost. Same BTU, same burn time. Easy training. Order a case or a pallet, no bulk commitment.
- Value. Performance. Efficiency. High performance should not come at the expense of high costs. More heat, less fuel, lower costs. Prime 6 provides a steady, consistent heat for 5+ hours with low ash and a clean burn, saving you money every step of the way.
- The prime 6 difference — Density / Process / Results
- It all starts with the raw material. Instead of random wood with unpredictable density, we compress sawdust into ultra-dense logs—no chemicals, no glues, no additives. Just one ingredient: hardwood. 3X denser than wood, with no air pockets or moisture, delivering the consistency needed for high performance.
- Precision matters. Traditional charcoal production is unpredictable—different wood sizes and densities lead to inconsistent results. Prime 6 uses advanced furnaces to control time, temperature, and oxygen levels, ensuring a precise process with uniform raw materials. This maximizes efficiency and achieves the highest carbon content without sacrificing density. More carbon = better performance. Every piece of P6 charcoal is the same high quality—no unusable pebbles, no under-carbonized chunks. Just pure, consistent fuel.
- Reliability in every log. Whether charcoal or wood, Prime 6 delivers the same performance—every time. Once you dial in your setup, P6 burns predictably. No fire management, no constant replenishing, no guesswork. Just consistency you can count on.

No moisture, no sparks, <5% ash. 100% hardwood – no chemicals, no additives. Efficient. Predictable. Cost-saving.
- Are you paying for pounds or performance? Charcoal quality comes down to two things: raw material and carbonization. With lump charcoal, you can’t control the wood species, density, or size—so the carbonization process applies the same heat to uneven pieces, like roasting a prime rib and a filet together. One gets overcooked, the other undercooked. At Prime 6, we engineer our charcoal. We extrude sawdust into uniform logs—no binders, no chemicals. Our proprietary furnaces precisely control oxygen, temperature, and time, ensuring every log is dense, carbon-rich, and burns consistently. Think of it like sous vide—each piece is identical, delivering predictable results. Higher heat = fewer LBs needed. Longer burn time = less frequent refills. More control = better performance.
